How it works

Two apps work together. You stay in control from your phone, and your child's phone does the enforcing, so you don't have to be the bad guy.

  1. Set the goal

    In Lockout Parent, add your child and choose a daily study goal, the subjects, a weekly schedule and how much free time they earn.

  2. They study

    Lockout on your child's phone serves reading passages with questions and math practice. Your PIN keeps the settings locked.

  3. Free time unlocks

    When the goal is done, the free time you set is theirs. They earned it, and they know it.
