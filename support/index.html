Getting started

Which app goes on which phone?

Lockout Parent goes on your phone. It's where you set goals, subjects, schedules and free time. Lockout goes on your child's Android phone or tablet. It shows the study work and unlocks free time when the goal is done.

How do I pair my child's device?
  1. Open Lockout Parent and add your child.
  2. Lockout Parent shows a pairing code.
  3. Open Lockout on your child's device and enter the code.

Each child can have more than one device. Premium covers up to 5 children and 15 devices.

Do I need an account?

Yes. A parent creates the account in Lockout Parent with an email address and password. Children don't need their own account or email.

Using Lockout

What's the difference between Free and Premium?

On the free plan, your child picks which subject to study. Premium lets you choose or rotate subjects, set daily goals and reading minimums, write or scan homework, import teacher assignments, and manage up to 5 children and 15 devices.

Why does Lockout ask for the Accessibility permission?

The optional study-time lock uses Android's Accessibility Service to see which app is open during study time. If your child leaves Lockout for another app before the goal is done, Lockout brings them back. It can't read the screen, messages, passwords or anything typed. You turn it on during setup, and you can turn it off at any time in the phone's settings.

The study-time lock stopped working. What should I check?
  • On your child's device, make sure Lockout's Accessibility permission is still on in Settings.
  • Turn off battery optimization for Lockout. Some phones, especially Samsung, close apps in the background to save battery.
  • Open Lockout Parent. It shows whether the lock is active and warns you if something needs attention.
I forgot my parent PIN.

On the screen where you type your PIN, tap the Reset button and follow the steps. If you're still stuck, email us from the address on your account and we'll help.

Are worksheet photos uploaded anywhere?

No. When you scan a worksheet, the photo is read on your own phone and never uploaded.

Is Lockout available on iPhone or iPad?

Not yet. Lockout is on Android today, and the iOS versions are in the works.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel Premium?

Premium is billed through Google Play, so you manage it there:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Lockout Parent and tap Cancel subscription.

Uninstalling the app doesn't cancel the subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. You can request one from your Google Play order history.

Deleting your account and data

From inside Lockout Parent, you can delete a child's profile, remove emergency contacts, unpair a device, or delete your whole account. Deleting removes the related data from active use.

You can also ask us to do it. Email hello@brightermindapps.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete your account, your children's profiles, their study progress and settings, and any emergency contacts you added, and we'll confirm by email when it's done.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play subscription. Please cancel Premium first using the steps above.
