Privacy Policy for Lockout

Last updated: August 6, 2026
Applies to the Lockout family of apps: Lockout (child app), Lockout Parent, and Lockout Teacher.

Plain-language summary. Lockout helps parents and guardians encourage study time on a child's device. Parents create the account and control the settings; the child's app enforces them. We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not show ads, we do not sell anyone's data, and we do not use children's data to build advertising profiles. This page explains, in full, what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

This Privacy Policy describes how Brighter Mind Apps, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Lockout apps and related services (together, the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Who this Service is for, and who controls the data

Lockout is a parental-control and study-enforcement tool. It is designed to be set up and controlled by a parent, guardian, or (for classroom features) a teacher — the "Account Holder" — and used on a device belonging to a child under that person's care.

The Account Holder decides what information about a child is entered into the Service, and directs how the Service is used on the child's device. We act as a service provider carrying out those instructions. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children except as configured and directed by the Account Holder for the purpose of operating the Service on the child's device.

2. Information we collect

Account information (parents and teachers)

  • Email address and password. Used to create and secure an account, through Google Firebase Authentication. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored by us in readable form.
  • Account type and subscription status (for example, whether an account is on the free or premium tier).

Child profile information (entered by the Account Holder)

  • A child's name or nickname, as chosen by the Account Holder. This can be a first name or any label the parent prefers.
  • A PIN used to protect the settings area on the child's device. PINs are stored only in a hashed (irreversible) form and cannot be read back by us.
  • Study settings and progress — daily study goals, free-time limits, study schedules, and how much study time has been completed.
  • Emergency contact details that the Account Holder chooses to add (for example, a name and phone number the child can call). These are provided and controlled by the Account Holder.

Device and enforcement information

  • Device pairing identifiers. When a child's device is paired to an account, the Service records an anonymous device identifier so the parent's app and the child's app can stay in sync. This is not tied to your name or email beyond the pairing relationship.
  • Enforcement status. The child's device reports whether the study lock is currently active and healthy (for example, whether the required permissions are enabled), so the parent's app can show it and warn if enforcement has stopped working.
  • The name of the app currently in the foreground on the child's device, used only in the moment to enforce a study lock — see the Accessibility Service section below.

Accessibility Service — what it does and does not do

On Android, the child app uses an Accessibility Service to enforce study lockouts. This is a sensitive permission, so we want to be precise about it.

  • What it does: while a study lockout is active, the service detects which application is currently in the foreground. If the child leaves the Lockout app for a non-permitted app during study time, the service returns them to Lockout.
  • What it does not do: the service is configured so that it cannot read the contents of the screen — not text, form fields, messages, passwords, or anything inside another app. It sees only the identity (package name) of the app in front. We do not collect, store, or transmit screen contents.
  • The Account Holder enables this permission knowingly during setup, and can disable it at any time in the device's system settings.

Classroom information (Lockout Teacher)

  • Teachers may create study assignments and share them via a code. When a parent imports an assignment, a copy is stored under that parent's account. Assignments contain the study content the teacher creates; they are not intended to contain personal information about specific children.

Payment information

Premium features are offered through a subscription. Payments are processed by Google Play Billing, and subscription status is managed with the help of RevenueCat, a subscription-management provider.

  • We do not collect or store your credit-card number, bank details, or full payment credentials. Those are handled directly by Google Play.
  • We receive confirmation of your subscription status (for example, active, renewed, cancelled, or expired) so we can enable or disable premium features. This confirmation is delivered through RevenueCat and a secure, validated server process; the "premium" status on your account can only be set by that validated process, never by the app on your device.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure accounts and pair devices.
  • To operate the study-enforcement features the Account Holder configures.
  • To sync settings and status between the parent's app and the child's device.
  • To enable and manage premium subscriptions.
  • To generate age-appropriate study content (see below).
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the Service, and to comply with legal obligations.

AI-generated study content

Some study passages can be generated with the help of an AI service (the Anthropic Claude API). The prompts we send to generate this content describe only a topic and an approximate grade or difficulty level (for example, "a short reading passage about volcanoes at a third-grade level"). We do not include a child's name, PIN, contacts, or any other personal information in these prompts. Generated content is reviewed before it is made available.

4. What we do NOT do

  • We do not show third-party advertisements in the Service.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to anyone.
  • We do not use children's information to build advertising or marketing profiles.
  • We do not use the Accessibility Service to read or collect the contents of other apps or of the screen.

5. How information is shared

We share information only with the service providers that make the app work, and only as needed:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Functions) — stores account, child-profile, settings, and status data, and runs our secure server logic. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes subscription payments.
  • RevenueCat — manages and validates subscription status.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — receives topic-and-grade prompts to generate study content, with no personal information included.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of users, or in connection with a business transfer (for example, if the Service is acquired), in which case we will require the recipient to honor this Policy.

6. Children's privacy

Lockout is a tool for parents and guardians to manage a child's device. Because the Service is used on children's devices, we take particular care:

  • Accounts are created and controlled by an adult Account Holder.
  • Information about a child is entered and controlled by the Account Holder.
  • We collect from the child's device only what is needed to operate the features the Account Holder has configured.
  • We do not knowingly use children's information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the Service.

If you believe a child's information has been provided to us in a way that a parent or guardian did not intend, please contact us and we will address it promptly, including deleting the information where appropriate.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Account and profile information is kept while the account is active.
  • An Account Holder can delete a child's profile, remove emergency contacts, unpair a device, or delete their account, which removes the associated data from active use.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below and we will process the request.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect information, including encrypted connections, hashed PINs and passwords, and server-side rules that restrict who can read or change data. For example, sensitive status such as a premium subscription flag can only be set by a validated server process, not by any app on a device. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to correct issues promptly if they arise.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Account Holders can review and change most information directly in the app. To make a request that you cannot complete in the app, contact us at the email below. We honor rights provided under applicable laws, including, where they apply, the EU/UK GDPR and U.S. state privacy laws.

10. International users

The Service is operated using Google Cloud/Firebase infrastructure and may process and store information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers. By using the Service, you understand that your information may be processed in these locations.

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
